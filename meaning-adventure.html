CONTRIBUTION QUEST

A story about surviving a fierce jungle by helping the animals who become your allies.

You can understand and speak with the animals. Ten choices, no right answers — what you do under pressure is what the jungle remembers.

Scene 1 of 10

Lost at Dawn

How the scoring works

No hidden weighting. There are ten scenes and five ways of helping. Each scene offers exactly three of the five, and across the whole story every way of helping is offered exactly six times — so no path is easier to land on than another.

Each choice adds one point to one trait. Your result is simply the count: if you are told you chose care four times, you chose it four of the six times it was on the table. The story is a mirror, not a test, and it is not measuring anything about you that you did not just decide.

Why these five ways of helping

The five traits come from recurring themes in psychology and meaning research.

  • Frankl: creation, experience, and attitude toward suffering
  • Coherence, purpose, and mattering as meaning components
  • Self-Determination Theory: autonomy, competence, relatedness
  • Erikson: generativity and contribution in adulthood
